This Skinpack was made by members of TAMS2SP - The AMS2 Skinning Project.

It includes 24 liveries and drivers helmets plus a coresponding AI-file to replicate the 1983 season:

Williams #1 Rosberg & #2 Laffite (by JoL)
Tyrrell #3 Alboreto & #4 Sullivan (by JoL)
Brabham #5 Piquet & #6 Patrese (by JoL)
McLaren #7 Watson & #8 Lauda (by Wolf)
ATS #9 Winkelhock (by JoL)
Lotus #11 de Angelis & #12 Mansell (by Wolf)
Renault #15 Prost & #16 Cheever (by JoL)
Alfa Romeo #22 de Cesaris & #23 Baldi (by ianez)
Ligier #25 Jarier & #26 Boesel (by Alain Fry)
Ferrari #27 Tambay & #28 Arnoux (by JoL)
Arrows #29 Surer & #30 Boutsen (by Alain Fry)
Theodore #34 Cecotto (by JoL)
Toleman #35 Warwick (by Alain Fry)
Spirit #40 Johansson (by AnnoyingSensei)

Due to the limitation of 24 skins in the game, we could not include the second Theodore (#33 Guerrero) and the second Toleman (#36 Giacomelli). You will find the skins in a subfolder, and if you know how to handle the AMS2 XML-files, you can exchange them for any driver as you like.

Humpty (aka mungopark) made an AI-file according to the 1983 results and balance of power. It was very tricky to balance the Brabham, McLaren, G3-Turbo and G3-V8 cars to get realistic results. To achieve that, he had to lower the AI-skills of the Non-Turbo cars. This leads to a relatively low average skill level in the pack. To compensate that, you can increase the general opponents skill level with the slider in the game.

Thanks to all the guys who contributed to the pack, especially Alain for making all the preview pics and encouraging me to start this.

Enjoy your trip back to the 80ies!
